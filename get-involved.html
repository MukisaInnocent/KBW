Get involved

Ordinary people making a real difference

There are three ways to join us.

Volunteer

Help with collections, sorting, distribution, outreach, design or admin. Every volunteer completes an application, gives two referees and signs our safeguarding agreement.

Ask to volunteer

Partner with us

Schools, businesses and faith communities can host a collection drive, offer storage or transport, or sponsor a distribution day.

Start a conversation

Refer someone

Know a household in need? Local leaders, schools and health workers can refer people to us. Requests are handled in private and with respect.

What we do

Five ways we meet basic needs

Practical support for vulnerable people, given with dignity.

Clothing Support

Clean clothes, shoes, blankets and school uniforms.

Food Packages

Small packs of staple, non-perishable food for households.

Dignity & Hygiene Kits

Soap, toothpaste and brushes, sanitary pads and basic toiletries.

School Support

Exercise books, pens, school bags and uniforms so children can stay in class.

Other Essentials

Bedding, cooking utensils and more, agreed case by case by our team.

Who we serve

Families on very low income, orphans and vulnerable children, elderly people living alone, people with disabilities, and those affected by disaster or displacement.

Our values

What guides every distribution

Dignity

Respect for everyone, given privately and without judgement.

Compassion

We listen first and respond to real needs.

Inclusion

Open to all, whatever their religion, ethnicity or nationality.

Transparency

Honest records of what we receive and where it goes.

Safety

Written safeguarding rules protect children and vulnerable adults.

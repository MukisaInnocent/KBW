What we need most

Please give items that are clean, undamaged and in date.

Clothing

Children's clothes, school uniforms, shoes, blankets, new underwear and socks.

Food

Sealed rice, maize flour, beans, cooking oil, sugar and salt.

Hygiene

Bar soap, toothpaste, toothbrushes and sanitary pads.

School

Exercise books, pens, pencils and school bags.

We cannot accept torn or used items, opened or homemade food, alcohol, or medicines from individuals.

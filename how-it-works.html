How it works

Simple, fair and fully recorded

Every donation is logged and every item given is documented.

1

Collect

Two team members receive each donation and a thank-you follows within 7 days.

2

Sort

Items are checked for quality, packed into standard packs and stored safely.

3

Identify

Trusted referrals and home visits in pairs confirm need, scored with a fair checklist.

4

Distribute

Help is delivered privately and respectfully, with no conditions attached.

5

Document

Records are updated within 48 hours and summarised in a monthly report.

6

Grow

We review feedback each month and build partnerships with schools, businesses and faith communities.

Our commitments

What we will always do, and never do

We always

Record every donation and every item given. Treat people with respect. Serve fairly using a clear checklist. Protect personal information. Work in teams, never alone with a child.

We never

Attach religious or political conditions. Sell donated goods or use them personally. Publish a photo, name or story without written consent. Give medical, legal or counselling advice.
